📡 3. WiFi Hotspot Portal (No PC Needed)

Configure WiFi & Server from your mobile phone without touching Arduino code.

  1. Hold the Touch Sensor for 3 seconds or reboot ESP32 and type r in Serial.
  2. OLED displays: JANSEVA_SETUP (192.168.4.1).
  3. Connect your Phone / Laptop WiFi to JANSEVA_SETUP.
  4. Open browser at: http://192.168.4.1.
  5. Select your WiFi SSID, enter password, and paste the Cloud Server URL.
  6. Click Save & Connect. The ESP32 connects and begins operating!
