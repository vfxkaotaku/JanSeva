📡 ESP32 First Wakeup & WiFi Setup Instructions:
  1. Power on ESP32 or hold touch sensor for 5 seconds.
  2. Connect phone/PC to WiFi: JANSEVA_SETUP_AP
  3. Open browser at: http://192.168.4.1
  4. Enter your Home WiFi SSID, Password & Server URL: http://localhost:3000
  5. Click Save & Connect. The OLED will show "JANSEVA.AI Ready".
